## 更新标题

3DPartHub v4.3.6 - tar 条目上限从 20 万放宽到 100 万

## 标签描述

3DPartHub v4.3.6 raises the backup archive entry-count sanity cap introduced in v4.3.5 from 200,000 to 1,000,000 tar entries. Each tar verbose listing line is roughly 80-100 bytes, so 1M entries ≈ 100MB of raw listing output — comfortably inside the 512MB maxBuffer (whose true hard ceiling is ~5M entries; the cap deliberately leaves headroom below it). Current real archives hold ~6,000 entries (1,319 models × ~5 files each), so even a million-entry ceiling is far beyond any realistic growth; the cap exists purely to reject corrupted/mistaken archives before they exhaust memory, not to constrain legitimate backups. No other changes. No DB migration.

## 更新描述

本次为**参数放宽**，无数据库迁移，无前端改动。

- v4.3.5 给恢复前的 tar 列表加了 20 万条目数上限（防损坏包撑爆内存的保险丝）
- 本版放宽到 **100 万条**：每条列表行约 80–100 字节，100 万条 ≈ 100MB 输出，稳在 512MB 缓冲内（硬顶约 500 万条，不取满）
- 当前真实备份包约 6 千条目，此上限只是异常包保险丝，正常使用远碰不到

**建议升级后验证**：无需专门验证，随版本更新即可。
