## 更新标题

3DPartHub v4.5.10 - 手机端模型预览图恢复满幅显示（修复 v4.5.9 留白观感）

## 标签描述

v4.5.10 refines the v4.5.9 mobile thumbnail fix. In v4.5.9 the mobile model card image slot switched from a fixed 140px height with object-cover to a 4:3 aspect ratio with object-contain to stop foldable phones from cropping previews into a thin strip — but object-contain also revealed the empty margins baked into the square thumbnail files, making every card look like it had white borders. This release restores the historical "fill the card" look: the slot keeps the width-proportional 4:3 aspect ratio (so cropping stays a constant 12.5% top/bottom regardless of viewport width — foldables no longer lose most of the image) while the image goes back to object-cover, which crops away exactly those baked-in margins. Verified at 375 / 430 / 540 / 717 / 767px viewports: images fill the slot edge to edge with no visible borders and no over-cropping. No server-side or database changes.

## 更新描述

本次为 **手机端预览图显示修复** 版本，无数据库迁移、无服务端改动（纯前端 2 文件 4 行）。

**修复：手机端模型列表预览图留白（v4.5.9 引入的观感问题）**

- v4.5.9 为修复折叠屏裁切，把手机端卡片图片槽从「固定 140px 高 + object-cover」改成了「4:3 比例 + object-contain」完整显示——但缩略图文件本身是方形（512×512）且四周有取景留白，contain 会把这些留白原样显示出来，观感上每张卡片都像有白边
- 本版恢复 **object-cover 填满**显示：图片槽保留 **4:3 比例高度**（跟随列宽等比缩放），裁切量恒定为上下各 12.5%——正好吃掉缩略图文件内的取景留白，观感与历史版本一致；同时折叠屏 / 平板宽度下裁切百分比不再随视口变宽而增大（旧固定 140px 方案在 700px+ 宽视口会把方图裁得只剩中间一条）
- 实测 375 / 430（iPhone 15 Pro Max）/ 540 / 717（折叠屏展开）/ 767px 五档视口：预览图铺满图片槽、无白边、无过度裁切
